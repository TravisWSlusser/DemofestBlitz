Who is Qlik?

QLIK TURNS DATA INTO DECISIONS

Qlik is a software company. Its products help organizations bring their data together, make sure it can be trusted, and use it to decide what to do next.

  • Data integration: moving data out of many systems and into one place.
  • Data quality: keeping that data clean and reliable.
  • Analytics and AI: dashboards, predictions and plain-language answers.
Founded in Sweden, 1993  ·  Based in King of Prussia, Pennsylvania  ·  40,000+ customers worldwide

Why is Qlik at Demofest? Qlik's sales enablement team built this arcade to train its own sellers. The questions in Side-Qlik Blitz are the ones Qlik sellers play.
